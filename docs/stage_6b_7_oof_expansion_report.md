## Executive Summary

In accordance with user authorization, the Historical Match Explorer's `STANDARD_T20` coverage has been expanded by generating delivery-level win probability trajectories for the **477 pending matches** from the 2022–2024 training partition.

In strict compliance with governance rules, **Option B: Five-Fold GroupKFold Out-of-Fold (OOF) Inference** was implemented with **fold-excluded calibration** to guarantee zero calibration-label leakage.

Key milestones achieved:
1. **100% STANDARD_T20 Coverage:** Complete delivery trajectories are now available for all **798 standard T20 matches** in the corpus (321 existing production predictions + 477 newly generated OOF predictions).
2. **Zero In-Sample Leakage:** Every delivery prediction for the 477 historical matches was generated by a model trained strictly on the other 4 folds, excluding all deliveries and targets belonging to the target match.
3. **Zero Calibration-Label Leakage:** For first-innings Platt calibration, five distinct fold-specific calibrators were fitted strictly using out-of-fold predictions and outcomes from the other 4 folds. The target fold's match outcomes never entered calibrator fitting.
4. **Preservation of Existing Artifacts:** All 321 previous production trajectories, showcase thrillers (50375 MI vs RCB, 50138 GT vs DC), frozen models, calibration artifacts, and raw datasets remain cryptographically identical to their preflight baseline hashes.
5. **Full Test Suite Green:** All **92 tests** in `tests/ml/` pass cleanly in **21.92s** with zero regressions.

## 3. Model Configuration and Approved Features

For each of the 5 folds, temporary fold-specific models were instantiated and trained exclusively on the 4 training folds ($M_{-k}$):

### 3.1 First-Innings Defense Model
- **Architecture:** Logistic Regression (`LogisticRegression(C=0.5, max_iter=300, random_state=42)`).
- **Preprocessing:** `StandardScaler` fitted strictly on numerical features of the 4 training folds.
- **Feature Set:** Approved 35-feature `INNINGS_1_FEATURES` subset from Stage 6B.1 (match state, causal pre-match ABC ratings, venue priors, toss, and resources).
- **Prediction Objective:** $\hat{P}(\text{batting\_first\_team\_wins} \mid \text{state})$.
- **Deterministic Boundary Override:** Wickets lost $\ge 10 \implies P \le 0.05$.

### 3.2 Second-Innings Chase Model
- **Architecture:** Logistic Regression (`LogisticRegression(C=0.5, max_iter=300, random_state=42)`).
- **Preprocessing:** `StandardScaler` fitted strictly on numerical features of the 4 training folds.
- **Feature Set:** Approved 39-feature `INNINGS_2_FEATURES` subset from Stage 6B.1 (target score, runs required, balls remaining, wickets remaining, required run rate, target pressure index, and pre-match anchor).
- **Prediction Objective:** $\hat{P}(\text{chasing\_team\_wins} \mid \text{state})$.
- **Deterministic Boundary Overrides Preserved:**
  1. Target reached: $\text{runs\_required} \le 0 \implies P \ge 0.99$.
  2. All out before target: $\text{wickets\_lost} \ge 10 \land \text{runs\_required} > 0 \implies P \le 0.01$.
  3. Balls exhausted before target: $\text{legal\_balls\_remaining} = 0 \land \text{runs\_required} > 0 \implies P \le 0.01$.
  4. Safe one-run chase: $\text{runs\_required} = 1 \land \text{wickets\_lost} \le 8 \land \text{legal\_balls\_remaining} \ge 30 \implies P \ge 0.99$.

## 4. Calibration Procedure & Fold-Specific Parameters

In compliance with the **Critical Calibration Leakage Safeguard** (Section 5 of authorization):
- The global production Platt calibrator was **not** applied to its own training matches.
- For each fold $k$, a temporary Platt calibrator was fitted on the raw OOF predictions $\tilde{p}_{1, -k}$ and targets $y_{1, -k}$ of the **other four folds**.
- The resulting calibrator was applied strictly to the held-out fold $k$.
- Second innings retained identity calibration (as justified by Stage 6B.5 natural calibration benchmarks).

## 10. Known Limitations and Methodological Warnings

1. **Retrospective Out-of-Fold vs. Forward Live Predictions:**
   The 2022–2024 win probability trajectories were produced via 5-fold cross-validation. For any match in Fold $k$, the model was trained on matches spanning 2022–2024 from the other 4 folds. While the target match itself was strictly excluded from training and calibration, some training matches in the other 4 folds chronologically took place *after* earlier matches in Fold $k$. These trajectories are therefore **methodologically pure retrospective estimates**, not sequential walk-forward simulations.
2. **Distinct Provenance in Explorer:**
   The Match Explorer explicitly labels 2022–2024 trajectories as `Available (YYYY OOF)` with blue badges, while 2025–2026 matches carry green `Available (2025 Val / 2026 Test)` badges, preventing users from conflating retrospective evaluations with forward operational forecasts.
3. **Excluded Matches:**
   The 85 excluded matches (DLS, Super Overs, washouts) remain intentionally unsupported and unrendered, preventing domain-invalid extrapolations.
